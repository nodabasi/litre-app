Privacy Policy

What stays on your phone, what is sent

Effective 20 October 2026 · the Fillup mobile app for iOS and Android (shown on your phone as Litre)

In short

Fillup has no accounts and no server of ours. Your cars, fill-ups, expenses and notes are stored only on your phone; none of them is sent to us or to anyone else.

Data that leaves the phone goes to three places, all of them Google services: anonymous statistics on how the app is used (Firebase Analytics), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and ads (Google AdMob). You can turn the first two off in Settings. Below is exactly what each one sends.

What stays on your phone

  • Your cars — the name you gave each one and its fuel type.
  • Your fill-ups — date, odometer reading, litres, amount paid, whether the tank was filled to full, and your note.
  • Your expenses — date, category, amount and note.
  • Your settings — language, currency, theme and your usage statistics choice.

These live in the app's storage on your phone. With no account, sign-in or cloud sync, we have no way to reach them.

Backups: on iOS your records are included in the iCloud or computer backup that covers app data. On Android the app's backup is turned off, so records do not move with you when you change phones or remove the app.

Receipt scanning, camera and photos

The Scan receipt button on the fill-up screen lets you take a photo of a receipt or choose one from your library; the app reads the litres and amount from it and fills them into the form. If camera permission is needed, it is asked for only when you press that button; when choosing from the library, only the photo you pick is given to the app.

  • Text recognition runs on the phone with Google ML Kit. The photo and the text on it are never uploaded to a server.
  • The app does not add the photo to your records; once it has been read, only the numbers in the form remain. The temporary copy used for reading sits in the app's cache and is cleared by the system. A photo you take with the camera is not added to your photo library.
  • According to Google, ML Kit may send technical metrics such as device model, operating system, app information and error codes to Google to measure its own performance. These metrics do not include the photo or what is on the receipt.

If you never use receipt scanning, camera and photo permissions are never requested.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To improve the app we use Firebase Analytics to measure which screens are opened and which actions are taken. What is sent:

  • The names of the screens you open.
  • The fuel type when a car is added; whether a fill-up was a full tank and how many fill-ups there are in total; the category when an expense is added; that a record was deleted; your answer to the rating question.
  • The app language and the currency you chose.
  • What Firebase adds by itself: a random identifier for this installation of the app, device model, operating system and app version, and a country and city level location derived from your IP address.

Never sent: amounts, odometer readings, litres, car names and notes. Your advertising identifier is not given to Analytics and no user identifier that names you is set.

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report: the lines of code where it crashed, device model, operating system version and a random per-installation identifier. These reports are used to find and fix bugs.

To turn this off: Settings › Privacy › Share usage statistics. When it is off, Analytics and Crashlytics stop sending data. The app works the same; this setting does not affect ads.

Ads

Fillup is free and paid for by ads. You see banner ads on the home and history screens; there are no ads on the entry forms. The ads are served by Google AdMob.

To show ads, AdMob reads some data from your device. It does not pass through us; it goes straight to Google:

  • Your advertising identifier — the Advertising ID on Android; on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking. A number unrelated to your name that you can reset in your phone's settings.
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it — to choose ads for your country and language.
  • Device information — model, operating system version, screen size, language.
  • Ad interactions — which ad was shown and whether it was tapped, to avoid repeating the same ad and to filter out fraudulent clicks.

Your fuel and expense records are never given to AdMob. You can read how Google processes this data in its ads data policy.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience, and sensitive categories such as gambling, alcohol and dating are blocked.

Your consent for ads

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form appears when the app opens and asks whether you want personalised ads. On iOS, Apple's "Allow this app to track you" prompt may also appear.

If you say no, the app works exactly the same; ads are not personalised or are not shown at all. To change your mind later: on iOS, Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, Settings › Google › Ads (on some devices Settings › Privacy › Ads). You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in the same place.

In-app rating

After you have logged a few fill-ups, the app may ask whether you would like to rate it in the store. If you say yes, Apple's or Google's own rating sheet opens; your rating and review go to the store, not to us.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single record with Delete inside that record.
  • Deleting a car deletes all of its records too.
  • Removing the app from your phone deletes all of your records.

Because records live only on your phone, we cannot bring back anything you delete. On iOS, your phone's own backup is the only way back.

Analytics and Crashlytics data is not tied to an account or a name. Google keeps Analytics data according to the Firebase project's retention setting and Crashlytics reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted.

Sharing

We do not sell any data. No data goes to any third party other than the Google services listed above. Firebase data is processed on Google Cloud servers under the Firebase privacy terms.

App stores

Fillup is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google collect their own download and usage statistics as part of running those stores, under their own privacy policies. We receive only anonymous aggregate reports.

Children

Fillup is designed for car owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK give you the right to access, correct and delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Your records are already on your phone, and you can edit and delete all of them in the app. You can stop statistics and crash reports in Settings. For anything else, write to us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app stores or sends, this page is updated before that version is released, and the effective date above changes with it.
