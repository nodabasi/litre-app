Records and numbers

Why is average consumption not showing yet?

Consumption is measured between two full tanks. Once you log a second full tank after the first, the average appears. When you fill the tank to the top, remember to turn on Filled to full.

Do partial fills skew the result?

No. The litres of partial fills are added up until the next full tank, and consumption is worked out over that whole stretch. Fill-ups before the first full tank are not counted.

I entered the wrong odometer reading and see a warning.

The app warns you when a reading is lower than an earlier fill-up or higher than a later one. Open the record from History and correct it.

How is cost per km worked out?

Fuel cost per km is measured between full tanks, like consumption: the fuel paid for in that stretch divided by the distance driven. Total cost per km adds your expenses: their total divided by the distance between the lowest and highest odometer readings on record.

Can I change the currency?

Yes, Settings › Currency. TRY, EUR, USD and GBP are available. This changes only the symbol shown; amounts already logged are not converted.

Receipt scanning

How do I use it?

On the new fill-up screen, press Scan receipt, then take a photo of the receipt or choose one from your library. The app finds the litres and amount and fills them in; check them before saving.

The receipt could not be read.

Lay the receipt flat and take the photo in good light, close enough for the print to be sharp. Crumpled or faded receipts may not read; in that case enter the numbers by hand.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Text recognition runs on the phone and the photo is not added to your records. See the privacy policy for details.

Cars

Can I add more than one car?

Yes. Tap your car's name on the home screen, or go to Settings › Cars, then Add car. New records are saved to the selected car.

What happens if I delete a car?

All of its fill-ups and expenses are deleted too. You need at least one car, so the last one cannot be deleted.

Deleting is permanent. Records live only on your phone, so we cannot bring them back.

Data and backups

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts; your records are stored only on your phone.

Will my records move to a new phone?

No. Records are not included in iCloud, computer or Google backups, so they do not move to a new phone. There is no export yet.

What happens if I remove the app?

All of your records are deleted from the phone. They do not come back if you reinstall the app.

How do I turn off usage statistics?

Settings › Privacy › Share usage statistics. When it is off, anonymous statistics and crash reports are not sent. Amounts, odometer readings and notes are never sent in any case.

Ads

Why are there ads?

The app is free and paid for by banner ads on the home and history screens. There are no ads on the entry forms. The ads are served by Google AdMob.

How do I change my ad consent?

On iOS, Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, Settings › Google › Ads. See the privacy policy for details.
