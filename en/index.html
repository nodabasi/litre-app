Fillup

Cost per km and fuel economy

Fillup is listed in the stores as Fillup: Fuel & Cost Tracker and appears on your phone as Litre.

What does your car really cost per kilometre?

Log your fill-ups and car expenses, and the app shows what you pay per kilometre, your average consumption and what you have spent this month, on one screen.

How it works

  • Add your carGive it a name and pick the fuel: gasoline, diesel or LPG. You can add more than one car.
  • Log every fill-upEnter the odometer reading, litres and total paid; the price per litre is worked out for you. Or take a photo of the receipt and let the app read the litres and amount.
  • Add expensesMaintenance, tyres, insurance, inspection, road tax, tolls, parking, car wash, fines and more, each with its category.
  • See the numbersFuel and total cost per km, consumption in L/100 km, a monthly spending chart and all-time totals.

Consumption measured properly

Mark the fill-ups where you fill the tank to full. Average consumption is measured between two full tanks, so partial fills do not skew it.

Your records stay yours

No account needed. Your cars, fill-ups and expenses are stored only on your phone; amounts and odometer readings are never sent to a server. Receipt scanning runs on the phone too, and the photo is never uploaded.

The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings. The details are in the privacy policy.
